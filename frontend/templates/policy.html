🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

Cake Fantasy Luxury ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information.

Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Email address and password (securely hashed) when you create an account.
  • Order Information: Items ordered, delivery address, and payment details processed securely through Razorpay.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, and browser type to improve our website experience.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill your cake orders.
  • To communicate order updates, delivery status, and promotional offers (with your consent).
  • To improve our website, products, and customer service.

Data Security

  • Passwords are encrypted using industry-standard PBKDF2 hashing.
  • Payments are processed through Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant gateway. We never store your card details.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at support@cakeshopluxury.com.

💰 Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

Order Cancellation

  • Before preparation begins: Orders can be cancelled within 1 hour of placing them for a full refund.
  • After preparation begins: Once our chefs start baking, cancellations are not accepted as ingredients are freshly used per order.
  • Custom cakes: Custom/personalized cake orders cannot be cancelled once confirmed.

Refund Policy

  • If you receive a damaged or incorrect product, contact us within 2 hours of delivery with a photo as proof.
  • Approved refunds will be processed to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.
  • Refunds for perishable goods (cakes) are only applicable for quality issues, not for change of mind.

How to Request a Refund

Email us at support@cakeshopluxury.com with your order details and photos of the issue. Our team will respond within 24 hours.

🚚 Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

Delivery Areas

  • We currently deliver within a 25 km radius of our bakery location.
  • Delivery availability is subject to your pin code — check at checkout.

Delivery Timeline

  • Standard Delivery: Within 2–4 hours of order confirmation.
  • Same-Day Delivery: Available for orders placed before 2:00 PM.
  • Scheduled Delivery: Choose a specific date and time slot during checkout for special occasions.

Delivery Charges

  • FREE delivery on all orders above ₹500.
  • A flat delivery fee of ₹49 applies for orders below ₹500.

Care During Transit

  • All cakes are transported in temperature-controlled packaging to maintain freshness.
  • Our delivery partners are trained in handling fragile items with care.

Failed Delivery

If you are unavailable at the time of delivery, our rider will attempt to contact you. If unreachable, the order may be cancelled and a partial refund issued.
